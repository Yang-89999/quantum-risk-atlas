PQC Benchmark Suite

Explore cryptographic schemes, known attacks, and reproducible resource-estimation benchmarks.

The suite organises four things that are usually scattered: what is known about attacks on each post-quantum scheme, the assumptions behind every resource estimate, the benchmark cases that pin a scheme, parameter set and attack to a cost model, and the evidence that a reported number can be reproduced. It is a project-level research framework, not a standard, and nothing in it is endorsed by NIST.

Security interpretation Reserved · no automatic classification

A NIST security category is a claim about the cost of the best known attack relative to a reference attack on AES or SHA, judged across several metrics. It is not a scalar that can be recomputed from one resource number. This table keeps the claimed category, the recorded estimates and their assumptions side by side; the interpretation column stays pending until a separately defined and validated methodology exists.
